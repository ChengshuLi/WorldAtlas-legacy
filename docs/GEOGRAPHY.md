# Geography and territorial evidence

This contract covers optional source research, hierarchy, geometry and native-grid proposals. A research-only assignment may publish scoped evidence or a recommendation; it does not authorize shared-boundary changes, imports, production writes or package delivery. A specifically assigned end-to-end repair owner follows both this scientific contract and the engineering/delivery contract for the exact assigned scope.

## Source and identity rules

Preserve the original source, vintage, role, license/terms, retrieval date, exact bytes or lawful extract, subject identity, method, uncertainty, adverse contacts and unresolved results. Cite the source actually inspected. A source name, count, metadata row, nearest line, current administrative boundary or structurally complete hierarchy is not by itself proof of territorial meaning. Do not infer legal attribution from proximity, merge incompatible reaches, or represent a narrower settlement/source unit as a whole location. Honor access restrictions and actual denials; never route around them through a different actor, format or endpoint.

Locations are indivisible reference polygons with stable IDs. Administrative ownership and the geographic hierarchy are independent. The atlas hierarchy adapts the published WGSRPD scheme; its botanical groups are not political units or historical administrations. EU5 counts are scale references, not quotas. Antarctica is excluded. Exact source inventories, licenses, active hierarchy, and prepared-location audits are in `data/administrative-sources.json`, `data/sources.json`, `data/world-index.json`, `data/hierarchy.json` and `data/granularity-audit.json`.

## Boundaries, releases and approval

Propose shared edges with all affected neighbors; never move an outer boundary unilaterally. Keep predecessor geometry, stable IDs, crosswalks, source identity and historical evidence. A geographic release is a versioned reference product, not historical proof. A partial review cannot certify a parent or region. Before an import, verify the accepted-main macro/regional gate in `data/research-geography-gate.json`, exact release, hierarchy, footprint and subject pins, the assigned campaign scope, and explicit current operation authorization. A prior approval receipt or open umbrella issue is not current authorization. Without those exact conditions, do source research and provisional staging only.

## Geometry and physical evidence

Use native source coordinates and the pinned canonical-grid rule in `src/native-grid.js`; preserve holes, original owner precedence, datum/vintage, full original operand set and all adverse contacts. Do not silently repair or simplify source geometry, alter grid resolution, or change ownership assignments to make a result fit. A physical-reference classification (land, water, climate, vegetation or topography) is not a political owner, legal boundary, observed historical condition or a license to add land. Uncovered cells and unsupported source subjects remain explicit.

Preparation, independent evidence review, engineering integration and publication are distinct. Follow current helper/schema contracts (`scripts/prepare-geographic-release.mjs`, `scripts/check-geographic-regression.py`, `scripts/evidence-quality.mjs`) rather than copying historic command transcripts. Scientific source and result receipts remain in their immutable data/research/coordination paths and Git history; a report is not recreated or repinned merely to update a guide.
