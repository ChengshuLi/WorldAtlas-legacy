# Geography and territorial evidence

This contract covers source research, territorial purpose, hierarchy, geometry and native-grid decisions. A research-only assignment may publish scoped evidence or a recommendation; it does not authorize shared-boundary changes, imports, production writes or package delivery. A specifically assigned end-to-end repair owner follows this scientific contract and the [development/delivery contract](DEVELOPMENT.md) for the exact assigned scope.

## Source and identity rules

Preserve the original source, vintage, role, license/terms, retrieval date, exact bytes or lawful extract, subject identity, method, uncertainty, adverse contacts and unresolved results. Cite the source actually inspected. A source name, count, metadata row, nearest line, current administrative boundary or structurally complete hierarchy is not by itself proof of territorial meaning. Do not infer legal attribution from proximity, merge incompatible reaches, or represent a narrower settlement/source unit as a whole location. Honor access restrictions and actual denials; never route around them through a different actor, format or endpoint.

Locations are indivisible reference polygons with stable IDs. The six reference tiers are location → province → area → region → subcontinent → continent. Administrative ownership and that hierarchy are independent. The atlas hierarchy adapts the published WGSRPD scheme; its botanical groups are not political units or historical administrations. EU5 counts are scale references, not quotas. Antarctica is excluded. Exact source inventories, licenses, active hierarchy, and prepared-location audits are in `data/administrative-sources.json`, `data/sources.json`, `data/world-index.json`, `data/hierarchy.json` and `data/granularity-audit.json`.

## Boundaries, releases and approval

Propose shared edges with all affected neighbors; never move an outer boundary unilaterally. Preserve stable IDs, old geometry, explicit old-to-new crosswalks, source identity and historical claims. A split or merge does not transfer old history by itself. `scripts/prepare-geographic-release.mjs` validates migration receipts and reconstructs the original footprint chain; `scripts/read-geographic-release-manifest.mjs` reads release identity and membership pins. A release is a versioned reference product, not historical proof; an incomplete branch review cannot certify its parent or region. Before a shared geography change or import, check the accepted-main gate in `data/research-geography-gate.json`, exact release/hierarchy/footprint/subject pins, assigned scope and operation-specific authorization. Without those exact conditions, do source research and provisional staging only.

## Geometry and physical evidence

Use original source coordinates and the pinned canonical-grid rule in `src/native-grid.js`; preserve holes, datum/vintage, complete original operand set and every adverse/outside contact. Maintain agreed owner precedence and the full land denominator. For a selected-bank change, compare complete old/new indexed native rows in `data/canonical-grid/current-startup/index.json` with `data/ownership-selection.json` and the authorized patch: prove exact additions, removals, holds and no loss or reassignment of prior owners. `scripts/check-effective-geographic-regression.mjs` checks selected release/source pins and full row conservation; patch-count or cell-total equality alone is insufficient. Do not silently snap, repair, simplify or clip source geometry; change resolution or ownership assignments to force a desired result; or use nearest distance as legal/source membership. Source geometry and canonical-cell comparisons are evidence of the stated method only, not automatic truth. A physical-reference class (land, water, climate, vegetation or topography) is not a political owner, legal boundary, historical observation or license to add land. Uncovered cells and unsupported subjects remain explicit.

Preparation, independent evidence review, engineering integration and publication are distinct. Follow current helper/schema contracts (`scripts/prepare-geographic-release.mjs`, `scripts/check-geographic-regression.py`, `scripts/evidence-quality.mjs`) rather than copying historic command transcripts. Scientific source and result receipts remain in their immutable data/research/coordination paths and Git history; a report is not recreated or repinned merely to update a guide.
