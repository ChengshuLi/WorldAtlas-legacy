## Primary authority and source-role findings

The source and owner-level work is in [`owner-instrument-matrix.csv`](owner-instrument-matrix.csv), with exact owner IDs/source IDs, original source labels, key and interval counts, research status, and remaining primary-record questions. It records 15 owner groups (1,102 keys / 842 intervals) with a bounded, named primary-source application and an unresolved exact boundary link; 21 groups (1,145 / 803) lack an owner-specific primary application in the retained evidence. **Those are research-coverage categories only, not disposition or refusal partitions.** Neither class changes the 2,247 denominator.

- **Federal Republic of Somalia:** Official OSAG Law No. 26/2024 establishes NIEBC and assigns electoral constituency delimitation, studies, consultations, base mapping, geospatial harmonization and recommendations. It has no adopted owner-specific district/ward schedule, map annex, coordinates or source-owner crosswalk. The official 2012 Provisional Constitution Article 49(4) assigns district numbers/boundaries within an FMS to FMS law with House of the People approval; Article 49(5) refers FMS borders to pre-1991 regions. Article 111E supplies a study/map/recommendation role, not unilateral owner-specific district authority. The 2024 amendment’s Chapters 1–4 scope excludes Articles 49 and 111E. Law 26 is not applied to the four Somaliland-labeled owner groups.
- **Puntland:** Official Gazette Law No. 03/2023, Article 21, preserves existing district boundaries and sets a process for new boundaries (presidential decree after Ministry study/recommendation, then House of Representatives approval). Article 5’s Boosaaso mention is a regional-seat listing, not a district boundary schedule. The six Bari and three Mudug rows checked have no exact owner-specific schedule/map/decree surfaced in the bounded records. A Mudug province string does not establish which FMS governs. These are concrete bounded gaps, not proof of absence.
- **Somaliland-labeled owners:** ERIGAVO, ZEILA, BADHAN and BUHODLE are kept separate. The targeted official Gazette endpoint redirected to an unrelated host and was stopped; secondary Law No. 23 leads do not establish official promulgation or a current schedule/map. No Federal Republic boundary act is applied to these rows.

Official source references: [OSAG Law 26/2024 bulletin](https://www.osagsomalia.com/en/resources/bulletin-contents/details/legislation/2024/sharciga-dhisidda-guddiga-madaxibannaan-ee-doorash/), [Official Bulletin PDF](https://www.osagsomalia.com/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2024/Sharci_Lr.26_.pdf), [Parliament-hosted Provisional Constitution](https://parliament.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/simple-file-list/Dastuuro/Dastuurka_ku_meelgaarka_SOM_03092012-1.pdf), [Puntland Gazette Law 03/2023](https://puntlandparliament.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/XEER-MAAMULKA-GOBOLADDA.pdf).
