## Deciding evidence gap

A per-key source-role decision would require an authenticated mapping from each original source-contact identity to a named source feature and the relevant TGO ADM2 target, with the source vintage/identity and applicable terms established. For any legal-boundary conclusion, an applicable authoritative instrument and point-usable boundary representation would also be needed. The retained packet does not contain those associations. This assessment therefore supports documenting the 436 as unresolved, while making no claim that external research is exhaustive or that absence of an association proves absence of an applicable source.
