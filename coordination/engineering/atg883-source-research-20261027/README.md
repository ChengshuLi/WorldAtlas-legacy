## Source-specific qualification

### Primary legal and government evidence

- The official *Parish Boundaries Act* (Cap. 304) describes Antigua’s parish divisions and states that they continue as delineated or described in a map/plan of Antigua and its dependencies made or published in 1852 by William Musgrave Shervington. The text names divisions and landmarks for Saint Paul, Saint Philip, Saint Peter, Saint George, Saint John and Saint Mary. This supports the civil-parish identity/history of the six Antigua parish features; it is not the incorporated map image or a machine-readable parish polygon. The law does not itself establish the exact seven-feature digital geometry or a boundary for Barbuda. [Official Cap. 304 PDF](https://laws.gov.ag/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/cap-304.pdf)
- The Government Information Service’s parish page describes Antigua’s divisions as earlier administrative units, says Saint George was divided from Saint Peter, and lists parish/dependency capitals while identifying Codrington/Barbuda Council offices. It corroborates names and administrative context, not coordinate boundaries or the exact present applicability of this GeoJSON. [Government Information Service — Parishes](https://gis.gov.ag/?page_id=130)
- The official 2011 Census statistical-tables book records parish-specific enumeration districts and says ED maps were generated with GIS and provided to enumerators. It gives an enumeration-district census geography, not parish polygons; an ED cannot be substituted for a parish boundary. I used this only for the published category and mapping-method context and did not import population data. [Statistics Division, 2011 Census Book of Statistical Tables I](https://statistics.gov.ag/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Census-2011-Book-of-Statistical-Tables-I.pdf)
- The UN SALB Antigua and Barbuda record identifies the national Surveys Division but lists no geospatial datasets currently available. This is a bounded repository observation, not proof that no national or other authority holds a relevant map. [UN SALB — Antigua and Barbuda](https://salb.un.org/en/data/atg)
