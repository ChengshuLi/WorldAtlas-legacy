## Target-specific CBS evidence already applied

The official CBS 2009 and 2010 Labour Force Survey definitions correct a categorical claim that CBS statistical series excluded the West Bank. The 2009 definitions distinguish six districts/15 sub-districts from the inclusion, since 1972, of Judea/Samaria/Gaza to characterize Israeli/Jewish localities and population; they note 2005 data predate post-disengagement evacuation changes and use “Judea and Samaria Area” as of 2006. The 2010 definitions include residents of Israeli localities there from 2006, but retain the six/15 official administrative units and define locality classes separately. Statistical Abstract 2010 reports Judea and Samaria separately (120 localities in 2009) and notes the area is not divided into natural regions. These are source-role facts, not a center-to-polygon crosswalk, sovereignty conclusion, or proof that the simplified ISR polygons represent CBS statistical districts.
