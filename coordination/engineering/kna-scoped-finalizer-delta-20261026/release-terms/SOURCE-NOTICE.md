## Change and method summary

The existing consumed simplified geoBoundaries payload was compared against the retained full-source comparator. For the exact residual 248-key St Kitts scope, 232 centers are interior in both versions; 16 are interior only in simplified and outside in full source, so those 16 remain held. The comparison found no boundary or other-feature hits. The prior independent physical/native preparation remains bounded evidence only; whole-feature issues 41039 and 41040 (extra reconstruction) and 41043 (missing reconstruction) are retained, and the physical-center diagnostic reported no requested-center witness rows. This is not a legal boundary certification or a full-cell coverage claim.
