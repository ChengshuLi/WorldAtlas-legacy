# LUX exact30 full-versus-simplified source application

## Question and scope

Does the exact same geoBoundaries release’s unsimplified LUX ADM1 source produce the same original LUX member hits as the simplified source already used in the complete original 624-key comparison, for only the exact prior-held 30 points? This is a source-member comparison, not territorial adoption, source/owner reassignment, native diagnostic, constructor, or credit. Original 568 accepted cells and the other 26-key ACT work are untouched.

## Method and result

The application uses the original pinned `native-linear-evenodd-first-owner-v1` scanner (`src/native-grid.js`, commit `5c1919dd1c7e89618398454220b57edf0fa388d2`, blob `add4cef02bb5a790f22a9078fdf9bd51679059d3`) and exact pinned native latitude table (compressed SHA `ab0becdda100da3473d7bb5d34ccda6510aa1c0490c407082072c7201ad1e147`; decoded SHA `66db3d02ede56a75e9c58426ad1388552be3bf7e5e4477476f198983b7436d23`). It expands only the exact 30 original prior-hold keys. It does not rerun the 624 source comparison or retest other source families. Their exact original comparison reports all30 as LUX-only; those original non-LUX no-hit records remain pinned.

Results: original simplified geometry hits LUX for all30; full resolution hits LUX for2 and misses28. There are zero full-resolution native-boundary ties. Of the five keys previously inside SIG-GR but outside ACT, one remains a full LUX hit and four are full misses. Of the other25, one remains a full LUX hit and24 are full misses. The two full hits are one Clervaux feature and one Esch-sur-Alzette feature; their identity IDs are retained per key in the JSON result. There are no full-resolution foreign-family hits claimed or tested.

The full product is a more detailed same-release representation, not a present-day legal authority and not proof the simplified data are wrong for the original contract. This application demonstrates that source generalization itself is material for this exact residual: 28 of the30 historical LUX-only point contacts are not present in the same-release full-resolution LUX geometry. The two remaining full hits still conflict with the ACT/SIG-GR representation results (ACT excludes all30; SIG-GR includes five). None of these source variants alone changes the recorded owner or establishes current territorial membership.
