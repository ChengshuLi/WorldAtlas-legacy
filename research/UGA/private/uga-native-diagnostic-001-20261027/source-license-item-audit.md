## Result

For this 137-unit UGA ADM3 product, the retained metadata records a combined CC0 1.0 claim and an ambiguous “second license is Public Domain” detail for two repeated Wikimedia Commons source labels. The source-catalog mirror preserves these fields but contributes no item-level license or notice. Separately, the retained geoBoundaries product notice requires CC BY 4.0 attribution to geoBoundaries and citation of each file's metadata-listed sources.

The exact underlying item-level artifact still missing is the pair of Commons file-page records (titles/URLs and revisions) that geoBoundaries used, with their per-item license/author notices and an explicit mapping to source entry one and source entry two. No statement here treats the metadata claims as independently verified legal facts, expands them to unrelated inputs, or decides their legal effect.
