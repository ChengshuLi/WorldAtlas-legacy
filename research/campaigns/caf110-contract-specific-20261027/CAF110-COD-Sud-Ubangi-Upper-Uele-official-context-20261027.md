## Official Ministry of Interior page

The DRC Ministry of Interior’s “Les provinces de la République Démocratique du Congo” page is <https://interieur.gouv.cd/ministere/territoires> (accessed 27 October 2026). The page lists 26 provinces and their capitals, including Haut-Uélé/Isiro and Sud-Ubangi/Gemena (page text lines 17–25 and 118–171 in the retrieved page). This establishes current official nomenclature and administrative level.

For the map, the same page says “Limites ADM1 : geoBoundaries (ODbL, sources OpenStreetMap)” and describes the traces as open data “à titre indicatif”; it says official texts prevail and the map is pedagogical, not a replacement for decrees, official maps, or Journal officiel publications (lines 22–30). The page does not state a geoBoundaries release, represented year, source feature IDs, or a link from those displayed boundaries to the 2017 COD ADM1 payload consumed in the CAF comparison. Its explicit disclaimer means its map cannot be treated as a legal boundary instrument.

The Ministry’s official “Textes juridiques” page, <https://interieur.gouv.cd/documentation/textes-officiels> (accessed 27 October 2026), says laws, decrees, and regulatory texts will be progressively collected there; for texts in force it directs readers to the Journal officiel or the Ministry’s Directorate of Legal Affairs (page text lines 18–23). The page did not provide a specific dated provincial-boundary act or map in the inspected content. A bounded official-domain search for Law 15/006 / province limits did not return an official instrument body. Search results surfaced a copy of Organic Law 15/006 on Leganet, a non-government legal archive; it was not treated as a primary-source read or used to establish boundary facts.
