It reports that Western Equatoria field mapping was completed over a year before enumeration and that new villages were missing from enumeration-area maps (PDF p. 5).

This is official historical county/payam and census/electoral context. The report does not provide a Tambura administrative boundary map, legal delimitation order, current boundary dataset, 2020 source-feature identifier, or spatial crosswalk. Its explicit warning that census boundaries were not for administrative use prevents using the census tables as present administrative-boundary proof. It therefore does not resolve whether, or how, the retained 2020 Tambura target and CAF candidate features apply to these 266 keys.
