## Bounded disposition candidate

The material supports a finite, differentiated **researched-unresolved** proposal for these exact 143 keys as a candidate for root review: each owner has direct official NEC role/name evidence, the yearbook adds an explicitly limited historical map application, and the held-key/source contact records are pinned and conserved. It does not support positive repair. The remaining issue is source applicability to the exact 2020 target footprint and the individual held keys, alongside the retained competing source contacts, physical/native holds, and source-use limits. Neither the NEC electoral table nor the yearbook supplies a feature-ID or key-level crosswalk. No legal ownership, current boundary, exhaustive-search, positive, accepted-status, completion, or credit claim is made.
