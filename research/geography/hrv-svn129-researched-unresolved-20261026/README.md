## Disposition and limits

This is substantive, source-specific evidence that the NUTS2 foreign contacts are confined to a narrow zone adjacent to GURS's 2024 Croatian-border representation; it is not a generic foreign overlay inventory. It narrows the issue but cannot establish which side is authoritative: the source NUTS regions are statistical/cohesion units; the DGU application establishes current expected local-unit membership; the GURS line is a Slovenian official border representation in a border whose field marking and bilateral status have the stated limits. Consequently it supports a **finite researched-unresolved** outcome for these 129 keys rather than a positive waiver. It does not establish exhaustion or require a universal legal certificate. Keep each SVN contact and every original HUN contact separate and unchanged. No positive assignment should be inferred from line proximity alone; any later positive candidate still requires the accepted source-evidence, exact original-source physical witness and native-constructor/current-bank controls for those exact keys, without replaying the unrelated 28,885 accepted subset.
