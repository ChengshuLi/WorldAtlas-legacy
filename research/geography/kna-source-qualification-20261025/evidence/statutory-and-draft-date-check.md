# KNA parish statute and draft-map date check

Date: 2026-10-25. Read-only check of the cited official Law Commission statute and the official 2026 Nevis working-draft PDF. No map digitization or geometry comparison.

## Boundaries of Parishes (St. Christopher) Act, Cap. 23.03

The Law Commission PDF is a revised edition “showing the law as at 31 December 2002,” prepared under the Law Revision Act 1986. Its consolidation line reads **“Act 6 of 1773 … in force 1733”** ([official PDF, pp. 1–2](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act02and09TOC/Ch-23_03-Boundaries-of-Parishes-St_-Christopher-Act.pdf)). The text itself supplies no correction or source note resolving that internal date conflict. “Revision Date: 31 Dec 2002” is the consolidation cutoff, not an effective boundary date. The Act body contains no “effective” or “commencement” provision and no map reference. I found no way in this primary text to determine which year is correct or when the described boundaries took effect.

The Act does provide named statutory descriptions for **nine St. Christopher parishes**: §§2–10 describe St. George, Basseterre; St. Peter, Basseterre; St. Mary, Cayon; Christ-Church, Nichola-Town; St. John, Capisterre; St. Paul, Capisterre; St. Anne, Sandy-Point; St. Thomas, Middle-Island; and Trinity, Palmeto-Point. These descriptions use shorelines, guts, plantations and historical landholders rather than coordinates or a map. They establish legal textual extents in the consolidated text, but do not themselves give a machine-joinable polygon. The Act does not concern Nevis’s five parish features.

## Nevis working draft’s own publication

The [NIA “Map Showing Proposed Village & Locality Boundaries” PDF](https://nia.gov.kn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Settlement-Map-3-Draft-1.pdf) identifies the parish lines but calls itself a working draft for review, says all boundaries/features are subject to verification and modification, and gives a creation date of November 2025 (PDF text at page 1). Its PDF contains no embedded URI/link annotations, so it links no final/adoption successor from the draft itself. The official file therefore remains a preliminary map in this check; it supplies neither an adoption date nor an effective parish-boundary instrument.

## Result and limit

The primary statutory source provides nine named St. Christopher parish descriptions but no effective date or map; the 1773/1733 conflict remains unresolved. The official Nevis draft itself links no final successor and explicitly remains provisional. No claim about a final Nevis boundary source, the 305-cell inventory, or any spatial match follows from this bounded review.
