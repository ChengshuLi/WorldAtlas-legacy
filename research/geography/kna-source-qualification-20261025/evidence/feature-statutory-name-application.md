# KNA: statutory-name application to the frozen 10-feature scope

Date: 2026-10-25. This applies only the already-published frozen original feature/key inventory to the primary statutory text and previously checked NIA draft. It does not perform a geometry join, re-open source searches, or make a qualification/credit claim.

## Basis and interpretation

The frozen inventory is `research/KNA/original-key-inventory.json`, sourced from the retained disposition at commit `775f0a1eddf8964b81d217d01a801c2b338db9c6` and original queue commit `2d1fc6f430e756498908bf2c4e267356a13f5cc7`. It contains 305 keys across ten original source features; feature IDs below are the full `gb:KNA:ADM1:<shapeID>` identifiers and key counts were recomputed from these saved rows.

The [Law Commission's Boundaries of Parishes (St. Christopher) Act, Cap. 23.03](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act02and09TOC/Ch-23_03-Boundaries-of-Parishes-St_-Christopher-Act.pdf) describes nine St. Christopher parishes in §§2–10. It is a revised text as at 31 December 2002, with the unresolved front-matter citation “Act 6 of 1773 … in force 1733.” The body provides textual extents for the named St. Christopher parishes but no effective/commencement date, map, coordinates, or machine-joinable spatial schedule. The date conflict means this edition cannot date the target geometry.

The official [NIA Nevis draft map](https://nia.gov.kn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Settlement-Map-3-Draft-1.pdf) is titled “Map Showing Proposed Village & Locality Boundaries.” It labels parish boundaries and five Nevis parish names, but expressly calls itself a working draft and says boundaries/features are subject to verification and modification. It provides no adopted effective date or machine-readable boundary schedule. Of the five named Nevis parishes, four occur in the frozen ten-feature scope; Saint George Gingerland is outside this target inventory.

## Exact evidence still missing

For the six St. Christopher matches, the Act supplies the legal names and textual boundary descriptions but lacks a resolved effective date and georeferenced rendition tied to the original 2021-represented source features. A usable match requires an authoritative dated map/vector or coordinate/parcel schedule and exact feature identity to those frozen source IDs. For the four Nevis features, the Act is out of geographic scope; the Nevis draft is not an adopted effective-boundary source. Required evidence is a final official Nevis parish boundary instrument/map with effective date and joinable geometry, plus an exact bridge to the original four source-feature geometries. Neither textual name equality nor the draft lines qualify any key.
