## What a contact would establish

An authenticated native result identifies a relationship between an exact cell and an exact frozen negative operand under the implemented predicate. It does not independently prove the political frontier, explain the source's construction, or qualify the exclusion away. The exact reported predicate and scope must be preserved when the new rows arrive. No new contact values have been inferred from names, center overlays or metadata.

Conversely, a proved lack of contact under that predicate removes the need to research that operand as the cause of that particular hold. It does not globally invalidate the source or establish sovereignty. Existing center-only observations are not substituted for the new native predicate.

The scientific question is finite: what does the actually applicable source claim at this segment, how was the export produced, and does substantive evidence qualify the particular exclusion or support a narrowly unresolved proposal? It is not a demand for universal sovereignty certificates or every unavailable original.
