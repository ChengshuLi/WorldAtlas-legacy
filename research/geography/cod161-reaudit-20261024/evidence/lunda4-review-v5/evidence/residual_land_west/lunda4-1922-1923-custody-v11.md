# Lunda4: 1922–1923 survey records and a specific Kasai map lead

Researcher: 01a16f2e-19a2-7470-acfd-59b39f3e90d1. Date: 2026-10-24. Source research only. All four Lunda cells, and all 32 western cells, remain pending and held. No geometry was run, no constructor was applied, and no acceptance or accounting changed. This addendum supplements v9; it does not certify the earlier proposal.

The principal new result is an identifiable Kasai survey map at AfricaMuseum: BE-RMCA-EARTHS-004579, sheet 1 of an assembly of three, attributed to Pereira and Weber, dated 1924. Its catalogue extent includes all four retained cell centers. The explicitly linked 497×682 public preview was downloaded and visually read: it shows a river survey with a graticule, but labels and fine survey details are too small to support a defensible exact binding. The record lists hardcopy distribution, an empty digital distribution list and no associated resources. A full-resolution or physical reading remains a concrete finite next step. This is a custody result, not map-cell completion.

## Four original records kept separate

Actual retained Jentgen pages 75–76 identify the commission (Weber and José Maria Martins Pereira), dates and 34-marker sequence. Page 76 footnote refers only to Ministry of Colonies archives; it gives no journal volume, page, file number or original table. No official volume/page can honestly be derived from that footnote alone.
