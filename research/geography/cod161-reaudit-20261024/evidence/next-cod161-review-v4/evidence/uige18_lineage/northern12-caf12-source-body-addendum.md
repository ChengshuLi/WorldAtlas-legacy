## Findings that change the earlier assessment

The original CAF ADM1 source is no longer merely a candidate inferred from year and licence. The newly retrieved original archive's meta.txt and screenshot explicitly identify `Central_African_Republic_prefectures_blank.png`. I independently rehashed both shared archives and read the relevant DBF rows and construction records. No archive was downloaded twice, and no geometry operation ran.

The exact [Commons source image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Central_African_Republic_prefectures_blank.png) is now read as an actual image. The page describes a blank map intended for labeling, credits Golbez, gives an original upload date of 5 April 2006 and provides no machine-readable underlying source. The downloaded PNG is 2,000×1,366, 21,472 bytes; SHA256 `7b54ac5655a52634fb59ccb8b11c408ed576c6c0a689cfb0cff24f500cbaf65b`. Its SHA1 exactly matches the page's `cb0ba5139be0f08605d0ba9d51b0d254fb338de9`. The one-source approved download completed, session39672 exit0. There was no prior denial for this object.

The full image visibly contains black country/prefecture outlines on white. It contains no place names, coordinate grid, scale, river-channel depiction, legend, control points or method note. Consequently the image itself does not identify a main-channel, bank, median or other river-edge construction. This is a source-relative limitation, not evidence that its outline is inaccurate. No pixels were georeferenced, traced or converted into geometry in this research.

The CAF ADM1 XML records polyline creation, FeatureToPolygon, attribute work and export on **14 November 2022**. The archive's **2006 represented year** is a different fact. Neither date proves when the international river boundary was surveyed or adopted. Polygon construction and source naming do not supply the missing geographic registration or edge convention.

CAF ADM2's original meta.txt expressly states that its **2015 UNHCR document32968 PDF was georeferenced and digitized**. January11–12,2021 polygon creation/attribute/export records describe the derivative's construction. This confirms a raster/PDF digitization lineage rather than an authenticated original UNHCR vector transfer. No control-point, registration-transform or residual file appears in the archive manifest inspected here.

I also visually read the shared UNHCR map render. The dated9June2015 map names relevant sub-prefectures and distinguishes international, undetermined and sub-prefecture boundaries in its legend. It has a50km scale bar, but no visible coordinate grid or river-channel rule. A scale bar cannot supply the absent registration residuals. Its endorsement disclaimer is not proof of inaccurate cartography. The archive's capturedCCBY3.0 and a later portal default are separate temporal licence records; neither resolves border applicability.
