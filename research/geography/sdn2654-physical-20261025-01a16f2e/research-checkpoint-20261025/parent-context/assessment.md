## Scientific materiality for the existing result

For the expressly scoped result—native membership in the existing frozen ADM2
subjects, with complete189 same-family originals, full neighboring territorial
families and actual physical qualification—the omission of ADM1 from territorial
negative predicates does not by itself demonstrate a scientific defect. Expected
parent overlap is structurally compatible with a locality. Treating all19 states
as competing localities would change the question and mechanically penalize
ordinary hierarchy, without supplying a source-specific contrary relation.

This conclusion is not a blanket contextual waiver. All19 remain retained and no
measured ADM1 operand has been suppressed. No exact known contradictory parent at
a qualified key has been found in the existing evidence. The one missing record
at `[39473,121315,147277]` remains a deciding relation for that held Geneina key,
alongside its actual Ouaddaï interior contact. Its omission cannot be repaired by
majority support from the other Geneina rows.

The parent evidence **is material** to a stronger claim that the output certifies
all state boundaries, exact whole-locality nesting, settled sovereignty, ordinary
statutory status for Abyei, or conformity to an adopted award. The completed
qualification does not make those claims. Review should preserve the narrower
source meanings and explicit hierarchy limit rather than retroactively describe
this run as a nineteen-parent coverage test. A future actual nonparent conflict or
an explicitly requested containment guarantee would create a concrete additional
question, with its exact source/target/key and rule; it would not justify silently
waiving every higher-level operand or rerunning an unrelated full package.

No genuinely unread, accessible, feature-specific primary passage in these
retained packets was identified that currently decides a contrary relation among
the33. ADM1 XML documents removal of administrative-code fields; the final ADM2
DBF does not retain parent codes, without its own XML explaining that loss. This
limits a code-based crosswalk, not evidence of a different parent. Source-specific
construction/containment could be investigated if a concrete adverse relation or
stronger claim requires it. Reopening denied HDX/PCA/UN routes, repeating generic
boundary searches or demanding universal legal certificates would not answer the
present scoped question.
