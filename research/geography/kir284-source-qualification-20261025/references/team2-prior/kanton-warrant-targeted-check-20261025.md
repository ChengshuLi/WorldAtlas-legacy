The accessible index therefore did not yield a Kanton warrant/Gazette entry; its visible year range is not evidence that older Gazette records are absent.

The [Local Government Act 1984 text](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/kir74395.pdf) identifies warrants as the relevant instrument: s.3 provides for establishment by warrant and s.4 requires the warrant to define the council area's limits. It supplies neither a Kanton warrant nor its map/schedule.
