We therefore do not convert it into a positive repair; we preserve the exact source-relative contradiction as unresolved.

Official national measures show administrative change, but do not resolve this full-versus-simplified contradiction or bind every key: the 2022 Mejlis resolution describes named abolitions, status changes and annexed area transfers; the 2025 resolution establishes/re-establishes units by named predecessor areas and orders later descriptions for several territories. The 2022 census tables establish names/hierarchy, not boundary geometry. A same-name town/city is not proof that the old source feature was that settlement, and a later abolition alone does not prove an older source polygon wrong. In particular, the 2009-era Avaza tourist-zone use and the separate 2013 Avaza/Kenar etrap resolution illustrate why name alone cannot establish the feature's role. The exact applicable source role and any annex geometry are not inferred here.
