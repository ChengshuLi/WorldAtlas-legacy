The feasible CBS2020 map application has now been performed as a conditional documentary comparison. It supplies useful location and source-construction evidence but does not decide Be'er Sheva administrative membership for any of the three whole native subjects. The limit is concrete: the conditional locations coincide with or immediately adjoin an unlabeled cyan lake line, and the map's named layer and CRS records do not establish that line's administrative meaning or an exact native-to-document frame tie. No source acquisition remains pending, and no retained map passage identified in this continuation was left unread.

## Actual controls, axes and representation

The unchanged official CBS PDF represents 2020 and was generated by Esri ArcMap 10.3.1 on 31 August 2021. Its printed map identifies New Israel Grid, a kilometre scale and distinct district, subdistrict, international, cease-fire and treaty-line symbols. The complete map and legend were already visually read; the relevant lake region was now rendered independently at 300 and 600 dpi using the installed bundled Poppler.

The page has a GEO measure under its viewport. Its four GPTS pairs were interpreted as latitude,longitude, with explicitly `always_xy` longitude,latitude degree input to the document's own geodetic-to-projected operation. Output is easting,northing in metres. Local page controls follow the PDF's stored signed BBox: its north-to-south y ordering is reversed, so a naive normalized box would invert the map. The projected corners are approximately E71172–300973, N377579–821102. Their order and image placement agree with the visible grid and north arrow.

Projecting those document controls with their **own** WKT and fitting an affine local-page relation gives about 0.247 metre control residual; leaving out one corner gives about 0.986 metre prediction residual. A bilinear projected-corner representation changes the three placements by under 0.000003 pixel at the original 2200-pixel render. These small values assess the numerical consistency of the rounded control representation, **not** source positional accuracy. They do not establish a sub-metre geographic boundary.

The installed source-reading runtime is Python3.12.14, NumPy2.3.5, pyproj3.7.2/PROJ9.5.1. Pyproj is the existing user-site installation, whose actual path is recorded; this is not a stock producer runtime claim. No packages or runtime paths were changed. An initial pypdf TextStringObject-to-pyproj TypeError occurred before any output; casting that existing WKT value to ordinary str corrected the local reader. It was not a source denial or a geographic retry.

The document WKT specifies a generic GRS1980 datum rather than a named realization. PROJ supplied no strict non-ballpark WGS84-to-that-datum operation; its only available candidate described an unspecified-accuracy ballpark geographic offset. That candidate was **not** represented as an authenticated datum transformation. Instead, the three existing native angular values were used explicitly as a conditional same-angular-value approximation in the document frame for broad visual context. No exact-side conclusion depends on it.

A specific CRS identity check also prevents an easy but unsupported shortcut: the document's scale factor is **1.00007**, whereas the installed EPSG2039 definition is **1.0000067** and names the Israel1993 datum. Other projection origins/offsets nearly or exactly match, but this does not justify replacing the document WKT with EPSG2039. No EPSG2039 transformation was run. This is a documented mismatch, not a demand for a universal datum certificate.

## Finite source-relative recommendation

The primary map opportunity is now reasonably tried, with genuine controls used and actual local symbol/readability limits recorded. It does not support promoting these three subjects to a positive repair based solely on parent hierarchy, absence of another foreign contact or zero per-key physical masks. Nor does it prove that Be'er Sheva excludes the water. The matching Southern parent remains a source-role candidate as previously reasoned, but the original subdistrict's water-edge applicability remains unresolved.

These three may now be submitted for **independent consideration as researched-unresolved**, retaining the original source/physical/contact records and this concrete failed qualification attempt. This is a recommendation only, not acceptance or an adopted exception. There is no unperformed obvious reading step left in the acquired CBS page/legend/control/layer bodies. A future deciding record could be the original dataset's attribute/definition for this exact cyan lake segment or a sufficiently detailed, explicitly scoped administrative lake-boundary source with an established application method. Those are specific information needs, not a claim that all copies are absent, a compulsory new worldwide search, or an impossible sovereignty certificate.

All three positions are in the already identified northern group above DS81. The southern1998 point list was not extrapolated; the already-read northern1994 sheet2's administrative/international distinction remains in force. A map-owner label here would express the reviewed source-relative administrative relationship, not sovereignty or current control.
