## CBS source-role correction and exact 11-key impact

An initial reading of the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) 2006 district map treated its six district fills and separate “Judea & Samaria” label as evidence that the six-district source role had no application anywhere in the West Bank. That inference was too broad. CBS's official 2005 account says it added Israeli localities in the Judea, Samaria and Gaza areas to characterize their population beginning in 1972, and its later definitions specify that the 2006 series covers Judea and Samaria but no longer the Gaza Area after the 2005 disengagement. See the CBS [2005 English report](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/DocLib/isr_in_n/isr_in_n05e.pdf) and [2009 definitions](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/doclib/2009/build_survey101208/pdf/e_print.pdf). The same-vintage CBS [2006 district map, prepared in 2007](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/DocLib/2007/shnaton58/all_israel2007.pdf) remains useful for the six named district roles, but its schematic map alone does not resolve exact points or polygon membership.

This correction does not alter the 11 Khan Yunis records. The consumed foreign input is the retained geoBoundaries/Wikimedia `ISR ADM1` simplified body in the pinned source corpus (release path at commit `9469f09`; registry represented-year claim 2006, six districts; compressed part SHA-256 `4ef84358ff0c8885bf857611a3cf2d537f33d7e2ecf9d34155778ff4b4e44989`, decoded body SHA-256 `28fe2d4b800046000a33ea66b473f5502a6b7a4ad9d587cd059d63d5a4ccc5dc`). The 11 exact Khan Yunis cells each retain the Southern District feature-ordinal-0 operand with `kinds: []`; the diagnostic has no interior witness for that operand on those keys. The other retained PSE ADM1 Gaza parent operand has its recorded interior witness and remains the only adopted source-relative hierarchy context. CBS's 2005 Gaza inclusion and 2006 Judea/Samaria-only statistical scope therefore add no contrary interior predicate for these 11, and do not replace or remove any operand. No 2005 Israeli geometry is consumed here.

The correction does affect interpretation of the separate held West Bank cohorts: some exact keys have positive native interior witnesses in the retained ISR ADM1 Central, Haifa, Jerusalem or Northern District geometries. The CBS material means those contacts cannot be dismissed under a blanket “Israeli district source is inapplicable in the West Bank” rule, while the 2006 statistic does not itself establish that any specific key is a recognized locality or identify that key's district polygon. Preserve the exact contrary contacts and all 54 current West Bank ISR-interior holds pending source-specific application to those operands. Do not generalize the 11 `kinds: []` result to the West Bank cohorts or make a sovereignty/legal conclusion.
